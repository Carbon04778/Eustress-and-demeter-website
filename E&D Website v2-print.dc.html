The readiness gate

Before anything is sold

Nothing goes to market until the business can carry it.

Expansion does not open on a calendar date. It opens when three things are true. If they are not yet, you will hear it from us long before you hear it from a franchisee who trusted the projection.

    Proven unit economics

    Profitable in more than one location, with a model a franchisee can underwrite and a bank will finance.

  2. 02

    Documented operations

    Everything a stranger needs to run your store correctly, written down and tested.

  3. 03

    Leadership with capacity

    A team that can support franchisees without dropping the stores you already have.

What stays yours

This is still your company. We just carry part of the weight.

Growth should not cost you control of the thing you built. Every decision about your brand stays yours to make, and everything we create belongs to you, documented well enough that your own people can run it without us in the room.

Our job is to make ourselves optional. Most brands still call anyway.

You keep the standards

We write down what you would never compromise, then hold franchisees to it. Nothing gets simplified because it's easier to teach.

You approve every owner

We qualify hard and bring you candidates. You decide who gets to put your name on a building.

You set the pace

Growth moves at what your team can support. If that means fewer openings this year, we'll say so.

Why we do this

A founder once told us the third store was the one that broke her heart. Same recipes, same signage. And it just wasn't the place anymore.

Nothing had been written down. Everything that made the first store work lived in her head and in the hands of four people who'd been there since the beginning. That's the work we do: getting it out of your head and onto paper, so it survives the fortieth opening.

07

Questions operators ask

Do we have to franchise to work with you?

No. Plenty of brands stop after Foundation or stay in Growth Partnership indefinitely. Activation is an option, not an obligation.

How is a restaurant consulting firm different from a franchise broker?

A broker's work ends at signing. Ours continues through onboarding, the opening, and the reviews after, and we built the systems the new owner is being handed.

What if our restaurant brand is not ready to franchise?

Then we tell you, and we do the readiness work first. We have asked brands to hold a year, and turned engagements down.

How long does a restaurant consulting engagement take?

Foundation runs about a quarter. Growth Partnership is twelve months minimum. Activation begins when readiness is met, not on a date.

When is a restaurant brand ready to franchise?

Three things must be true: proven unit economics in more than one location, operations documented well enough for a stranger to run the store correctly, and leadership with the capacity to support franchisees without dropping the stores you already have.
