03

Marks & rules

Sparingly — one per view
Seal

The one real mark we hold. 64px minimum, gold on cream or charcoal, generous clear space. Not a substitute for the wordmark lockup.

Hairline rule

The default separator. 64px under a headline, 28px inside a card. Gold on light, gold on dark.

Squiggle

Stands in for the site's hand-drawn squiggle under value headings. Replace with the real PNG when we have it.

Heart

Sign-off only — footer, thank-you slide, the last line of a proposal. Never decorative, never more than one.

Do

One mark per view, at the emotional beat — the closing line, not the header.

Don't

Squiggle under every heading, hearts as bullets, or an ornament on a pricing page.
