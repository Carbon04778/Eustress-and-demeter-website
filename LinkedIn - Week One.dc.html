The five readiness questions every founder should ask

Answer these honestly before you sign another lease.

  2. 02 Are training systems documented?
  3. 03 Can managers decide without me?
  4. 04 Is the guest experience consistent?

Five signs your restaurant is not ready to scale

If more than two are true, the next location will multiply the problem.

  • □ The owner still approves everything
  • □ Training is not written down
  • □ Guest experience varies by shift
  • □ Every manager runs the store differently
  • □ Reporting is not standardized
